About the Malave Family

Our Story

We are a family who has always kept a camera close. From Sunday afternoons to summer trips and new years at home, these are the moments we gathered along the way and did not want to lose.

Our Mission

This site keeps that record in one place — easy to find, easy to share, and safe to keep. We add to it as the years go on, so the next generation can see where they came from.
